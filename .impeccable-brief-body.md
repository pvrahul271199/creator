## Scope and mode
Whole Boardroom app. Mode: Operate. Solo creator, phone and laptop.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A creator's business is a schedule of flights: each collaboration runs across time with deadlines and money landing on it. The surface refuses the grey-card, big-number dashboard and draws work and cash on one shared month rail instead.
OWN-WORLD: Media-plan flight chart. Deep indigo shell rail (#1B1F4B family), cool paper ground tinted toward indigo, cobalt for active work, saffron for deadlines, green for cash received, vermilion only for overdue. Geist Sans plus Geist Mono tabular figures, fixed rem scale 1.125 ratio, dense tables, 6px radii, hairline borders, no shadows except overlays. Optional alternate theme: Graphite Console (graphite ground, hairline seams, accents for state only), switchable and secondary.
STORY: Visitor sees what is owed, what is due next and where each deal sits in time, in one glance, then acts: add a deal, issue an invoice, record a payment.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left indigo rail with text-only nav (Dashboard, Collaborations, Brands & Contacts, Tasks, Invoices, Payments, Settings) and the account menu at the bottom. Main: page title with primary action top right; a compact four-figure strip (Active, Outstanding, Revenue this month, Overdue) as a single ruled row, not four cards; below it the Flight Plan (month rail with a bar per active collaboration, saffron deadline ticks, green payment dots); beneath that two columns: recent collaborations table and upcoming tasks list.
FORM: Flight chart signature on dashboard, collaboration detail (its own bar, tasks and payments on the rail) and brand detail. Position 5 of the grounded list. seed: 10819b44.
SIGNATURE: Flight Plan rail; hover or focus on a bar shows deal, amount, dates; keyboard focusable. Motion: only 150-200ms state transitions.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
